From a pull request to a running pod

Six stops, one pinned image tag. Pick a stop for what runs there, what starts it, and what keeps it safe. Switch between production and samuel-dev to follow either path.

    After the roll, scripts/cirrus_watch.sh (with --env dev for samuel-dev) reports only what changed: web, latency, cache, pods, the CronJob. The watch-prod and watch-dev Claude skills read it on a timer, or once after a merge.
