A Bootstrap tooltip mid show/hide transition when htmx removes its trigger throws `Object.values(undefined)` from _isWithActiveTrigger — tooltip-init.js disposes on htmx:beforeCleanupElement, but it cannot beat Bootstrap's own queued transition callback.
