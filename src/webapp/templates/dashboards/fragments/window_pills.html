{# Lookback-window pills for an HTMX-filtered fragment. ── Why this exists next to date_range_picker.html ─────────────────────── `date_range_picker` is a plain `method="GET"` form that navigates the whole page, driven by static/js/pickers.js. That is right for a page whose entire content is the range. It is wrong for a card that lives inside a page and re-renders one fragment: a full-page nav would throw away every other fragment's state. So these pills write into the caller's existing filter form and submit it through the form's own hx-get, using `set-filter-submit` — the same CSP-clean handler the facet chips use (static/js/actions.js). No new JavaScript, and the window round-trips with the chips because they are fields of one form. ── Contract ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── window_pills(form_id, pills, days, custom=False, start_date='', end_date='') form_id id of the enclosing filter form pills ((days:int, label:str), ...) days the selected value, or None when a custom range is in force custom True when start/end are driving the window start_date 'YYYY-MM-DD' or '' — echoed back into the custom inputs end_date ditto The caller MUST render a hidden `days` field inside the form — that is the control `set-filter-submit` targets by name. The `start_date` / `end_date` inputs are emitted here, and bound to the form with the HTML `form=` attribute rather than by nesting. That is what lets the window controls live inside a re-rendering fragment while their state belongs to a form outside it: `form.elements` includes form-associated controls, so both htmx's `hx-include` and `data-clear-fields` see them. ⚠️ Do NOT also put `start_date`/`end_date` in the form — two controls of one name make `form.elements[name]` a RadioNodeList, and assigning `.value` to that silently does nothing. ⚠️ Class convention: `btn-outline-secondary` + `active` for the selected pill, never `btn-primary`. The jobs pills shipped with primary/outline-primary once and it read backwards — blue looked like the *unselected* state next to every other selector on the dashboards. ⚠️ An explicit range must OUTRANK `days`, and the route is where that is enforced (`_parse_activity_window`).
