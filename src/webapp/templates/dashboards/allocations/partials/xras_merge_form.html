Reconciliation in XRAS *is* a merge, so the remedy is to complete the merge that never happened. ── WHY NOTHING IS PRESELECTED WHEN THERE IS MORE THAN ONE CANDIDATE ───── Measured on real data: one human had two live XRAS identities differing only by email and organization — a university address and an NCAR-staff one. A name match picks between them arbitrarily, and merge DELETES the loser and moves the placeholder's roles onto the winner. So the safe key is email and organization, never the name, and the operator chooses. Deliberate deviation from the plan: the override is a plain text input, not an FK picker. A picker over SAM users would return a SAM username, and the merge target is an XRAS identity — the two are not the same namespace.
