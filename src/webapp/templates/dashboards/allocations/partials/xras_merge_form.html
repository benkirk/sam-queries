That endpoint returns 200 and silently drops the parameter; a button wired to it would be the worst kind, green and inert. Reconciliation in XRAS *is* a merge, so the remedy is to complete the merge that never happened. WHY NOTHING IS PRESELECTED WHEN THERE IS MORE THAN ONE CANDIDATE Measured on real data: one human had two live XRAS identities differing only by email and organization — a university address and an NCAR-staff one. A name match picks between them arbitrarily, and merge DELETES the loser and moves the placeholder's roles onto the winner. So the safe key is email and organization, never the name, and the operator chooses. A single STRONG match (rank 0 — the email resolves to it) is the opposite case, nothing to choose between, so it is shown read-only as the confirmed target (no radio); Cancel rejects it. A single WEAK match still gets a radio — a name-only hit is not trustworthy enough to confirm for the operator. The override is a text input, not an FK picker: the target is an XRAS identity, not a SAM username.
