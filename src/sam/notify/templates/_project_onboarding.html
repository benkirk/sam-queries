Take a look at the new SAM

SAM, the Systems Accounting Manager, was rebuilt from the ground up this year. Much of what used to take a help-desk ticket you can now see, and do, yourself:

  • Manage your project yourself — add and remove members and set your project admin: open your project in SAM.
    • {% if has_subtree %}
  • Move allocations between your sub-projects yourself, without requesting new quota — from your project above, use the Allocations tab.
    • {% endif %}
  • See where your allocation is going — usage against each allocation, how your burn rate compares with the time remaining, and a breakdown by user. Select any resource in the table above, or start from My Accounts.
  • Job history — every job charged to your project, by user, queue and date, with wait-time, job-size and run-time distributions. My Jobs shows your own jobs across all of your projects.
  • Disk usage in detail — for Campaign Storage: usage over time by user, your largest directories, and data nobody has touched in a long time. See My Data for your own files.
  • System status — Derecho, Casper and JupyterHub queues, node health and scheduled maintenance, no login required.
  • Documentation: ncar-hpc-docs.readthedocs.io.
