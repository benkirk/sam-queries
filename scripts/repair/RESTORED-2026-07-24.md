Batch 2 is the more telling of the first two: those 12 users appeared in the
first cut of [`STRANDED-2026-07-24.md`](STRANDED-2026-07-24.md) as
still-deactivated, were reactivated within hours of that list being produced,
and **every one of them immediately had zero live memberships**. Reopening an
account demonstrably does not restore its memberships — observed end to end
inside a single day.

Batch 3 confirmed it at scale. The re-scoped
[`STRANDED-2026-07-24.md`](STRANDED-2026-07-24.md) listed **27** users swept in
the 2026-07-16/17 window with Q2+ charge activity, still deactivated. IGA staff
reactivated all 27 on request over the afternoon (13 inside a 25-minute burst,
12:49–13:13; the last three at 15:21). **Every one of the 27 landed with zero
live memberships** — a clean 27-for-27 reproduction of the defect — and each was
repaired the same day. The batch was applied in three sub-cycles as the
reactivations arrived (`c`: 23 users/258 rows, `d`: `jingyuanf`/5 rows,
`e`: `deppenme`+`emeeker`+`gseijo`/30 rows), each with its own pinned-ID
rollback.

Each row below is one `(user, project)` pair whose `account_user.end_date` was
cleared back to `NULL`, restoring the original `start_date` so the membership
history is continuous. `Resources` is the count of `account_user` rows restored
for that pair.
