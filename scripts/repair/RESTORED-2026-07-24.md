## Notes

- **`collazo`'s name is shown as stored, not as it should read.** `users`
  holds double-encoded UTF-8 for this row — `first_name` is
  `0x46C383C2A96C6978`, i.e. `é` (`C3A9`) encoded as UTF-8 a second time into
  `C383 C2A9`, in a `utf8mb3` column. SAM renders "FÃ©lix" everywhere as a
  result; the name is **Félix Santiago Collazo**. Unrelated to this repair and
  left alone, but worth a separate look.

- ⁽²⁾ **Confirmed not isolated:** batch 2 turned up the same double encoding on
  `mauricio` — stored as "MaurÃ­cio Rocha", should read **Maurício Rocha**. Two
  hits in 24 users suggests every accented name in `users` is affected. Worth a
  dedicated pass.

- **7 of the 27 pairs are Lead or Admin roles** — the closure stripped project
  leads and admins of their own memberships, violating the
  lead-∪-admin-∪-members invariant that `Account._seed_members` maintains.
  This also *masks* the fault: `User.active_projects()` unions `led_projects`
  and `admin_projects`, so a lead with zero membership rows still reports an
  active project in the UI and CLI while having no resource access at all. The
  project member card additionally suppresses its partial-access warning for
  the lead by design. "Shows at least one project" is not evidence of health.

- **`yifanc` was included as a named exception.** She fails detection rule 3
  (`users.modified_time` equals her close instant exactly, so there is no
  reopen to point at), but she charged jobs on 4 of her 5 closed projects
  within 90 days — including on the day of the close — and she leads
  `UBUF0002`. Justification is recorded in the script header.

- **Not repaired**, reviewed and deliberately held back: `hojungy` (4 rows,
  NERP0001), `egerber` (3), `xumin` (1). All three are whole-account closes on
  open accounts, but with no post-close user-record update and no charge
  history at all.

- **`jpereira` is not in this set.** She was the index case and was repaired by
  hand on 2026-07-23 with fresh rows rather than by clearing `end_date`.

- **Rollback** was generated from the pre-image before the write and verified
  end-to-end against a same-day clone of prod. Twelve statements, one per
  closure event.

## Upstream

The defect is in the LDAP/IdMS-IGA sync, not in SAM: closing a user account
end-dates every `account_user` row, but reopening it restores only
`users.active` / `users.locked`. Tracked at NCAR/sam-ldap-syncd.

A further **39 users / 224 rows** carry the same signature back to January 2026
and were left for a separate decision.
