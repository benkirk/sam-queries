## Inclusion rule

A closure is flagged **accidental** by intersecting two independent sources:

1. **Account closure after the IGA transition** — a whole-account close (an
   `end_date` instant that ended *every* membership the user held) with
   `end_date > 2026-07-10`, and `users.active = 0` today with no live
   membership since. Closures before the transition are out of scope.
2. **PBS activity in Q2+** — at least one `comp_charge_summary` row with
   `activity_date >= 2026-04-01`. Someone running jobs this quarter is not
   someone who quietly left.

Neither signal is sufficient alone. Their intersection is the claim.

**Result: 27 users, 293 `account_user` rows.**

The filter is selective rather than a rubber stamp on the sweep: of the **87**
users closed post-7/10 who remain closed, **27** have Q2+ activity and **60**
do not. The 60 are consistent with genuine offboarding and are *not* listed
here.

One structural note: the 7/10 boundary does not actually bind. Every one of the
27 closes falls inside an **88-minute window**, `2026-07-16 22:59:34` →
`2026-07-17 00:27:17`. Nothing between 7/10 and 7/16 qualifies at all. Twenty
seven independent departures do not arrive inside 88 minutes.

## What changed since the first publication

The first cut listed **14** users who charged in July and remained closed. **12
of those 14 were reactivated the same day, between 11:21 and 11:34.**

Every one of them immediately landed in the broken state — an open account with
**zero** live memberships — and required a second repair batch of **129 rows**.

The remaining **27** users in the table above were then reactivated by IGA on
request later the same afternoon — 24 between 12:49 and 13:13, the last three at
15:21. **All 27 landed in the identical broken state**, and all 27 were repaired
(batch 3, **293 rows**). Across 39 same-day reactivations (12 + 27), the
reopen-loses-memberships behavior held every single time.

That is the clearest available evidence for the upstream defect: reopening an
account demonstrably does not restore its memberships, observed end to end
inside a single day. See [`RESTORED-2026-07-24.md`](RESTORED-2026-07-24.md).

Anyone reactivated off a list like this will land in the same state. Queue the
membership repair behind the reactivation rather than waiting for a ticket —
[`RUNBOOK-missing-projects.md`](RUNBOOK-missing-projects.md).

## What would help from the upstream side

For each row the question is just: **is this person actually gone?**

- **Yes** — no action; the closure was correct.
- **No** — they need reactivation *and* a membership repair, because the two do
  not happen together (NCAR/sam-ldap-syncd#3).

`UPID` and `UID` are included so rows can be matched against IdMS/IGA directly.

## Reproducing

Same detector as

with rule 2 inverted (`users.active = 0`), the close window narrowed to
`> 2026-07-10`, and an inner join to Q2+ `comp_charge_summary` activity.

Re-run it as a watchlist: anyone who **drops off** has been reactivated and
should go straight to the repair detector.
